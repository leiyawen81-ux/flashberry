Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains how photos, camera access, and basic information are handled when using this online photo booth.

1. Camera Access

This website may ask for camera permission so users can take photos directly in the browser. Camera access is only used while the photo booth is open. The website should not access the camera after the user leaves the photo booth page or closes the browser tab.

2. Photo Usage

Photos taken in the photo booth are used only to create previews, layouts, and downloadable photo strips. Photos should not be posted publicly, shared with other users, or used for advertising without the user's permission.

3. Photo Storage

Photos are processed locally in the browser and are not uploaded to a server. If storage is added later, photos should only be stored temporarily for generating and downloading the final photo strip.

4. Downloads

Users can download their finished photo strip to their own device. After downloading, the user is responsible for how the image is saved or shared.
